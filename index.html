What do I get with a free account? +

A profile, a deed log with up to 6 posts, and full access to the CivicSense social feed. Follows, likes, bookmarks, and notifications are all included.

What does PRO add? +

A PRO badge, unlimited deeds, gallery inclusion, a directory listing, the impact story builder, availability status, and priority support.

Is there a plan for teams and nonprofits? +

Yes. Team accounts put your group, your people, and your impact in one place. Business PRO adds a directory listing, team badges, and shared posts across everyone’s profile.

What is the Civic Feed? +

A chronological social feed for sharing good deeds, ideas, links, and works in progress. Posts appear in order from people you follow. No ads, no ranking, no reach games.
